Questions & methodology

What exactly is anchored on-chain?
23 Base EAS sprint attestations cover Sprints 1–23. They are recorded by pacta.base.eth in PactaCommitmentRegistry (multi-attest txs 0x8c53…001b (Sprints 1–12) and 0x1154…edbf (Sprints 13–16) and 0x882e…c9a4 (Sprints 17) and 0x620d…6420 (Sprints 18) and 0x4346…2072 (Sprints 19) and 0x2633…fa4b (Sprints 20) and 0xe556…4fab (Sprints 21) and 0xb206…cf64 (Sprints 22) and 0xc077…d1ee (Sprints 23)); each attestation commits to the sprint’s Ethereum block hash and summary SHA-256.
Is the data free?
Yes, and in real time. Every indexed record, the timelock radar, the briefs, /api/v1/index.json and llms.txt are free for vault curators, allocators and AI risk agents: no key, no delay and no paywall. Curators never pay.
What does the $5,000 clearance include?
For collateral issuers onboarding an asset or clearing a parameter_delta flag: a canonical block-pinned control-plane audit, a GitBook / config reconciliation PR, a Base Mainnet EAS Aligned attestation and a Curator Diligence Dossier (PDF + JSON) ready to paste into Morpho, Euler, Aave or Sky forum proposals, in 24 hours, for $5,000 USDC flat. Continuous Aligned maintenance is optional at $1,500 USDC / month. Settle by formal commercial invoice (PDF) or directly in USDC.
How do I reproduce a record?
Every record gives the contract, the call signature with all arguments (or the storage slot), the block and the canonical block hash. Copy its cast command from the inspector or the index and run it against any archive node.
What happens to exploit-sensitive findings?
Exploit-sensitive vulnerabilities are never published here. They go through the protocol’s Immunefi or Cantina bounty programme, or to a partner audit firm under a 15% referral partnership.
Do I need a wallet?
No. The inspector, the radar, the briefs and the JSON feed are public and read-only. No wallet is needed for a clearance either: a commercial invoice (PDF) is the default; direct USDC settlement is optional.
How fresh is the radar?
The radar states the exact finalized block it was refreshed at (#26,429,140, 2026-11-16 17:39 UTC); countdowns are computed from each operation’s on-chain ETA. An ETA is when execution becomes possible, not a guarantee that it happens.
What do the classifications mean?
Parameter delta: on-chain state differs from the documentation. Protective delta: a change that strengthens controls. Aligned baseline: on-chain state matches the documentation at the pinned block.
